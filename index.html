About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the group of Prof. Andrew I. Cooper at the University of Liverpool. My research lies at the interface of supramolecular chemistry and crystal engineering: I design self-assembled architectures — metal–organic cages and porous molecular crystals — whose confined spaces and dynamic structures give rise to function, from host–guest recognition and allosteric regulation to photocatalysis.

I received my B.S. in Applied Chemistry from Dalian University of Technology and my Ph.D. in Physical Chemistry from Sun Yat-sen University under the supervision of Prof. Cheng-Yong Su, where I continued as a postdoctoral researcher. There, my work centred on functional metal–organic cages built from M(ImPhen)3 metalloligands — their redox activity, stereochemical control and cage-confined catalysis. In Liverpool, I am exploring ionic clusters as building units for crystalline porous organic salts.

    xStack PXRD Viewer

    v1.5 · Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · MIT License

    A desktop app for viewing, stacking and comparing powder X-ray diffraction patterns — from raw instrument files to publication-ready figures in a few clicks.

    • Batch import by drag & drop — two-column text (.xy, .xye, .dat, .csv …), Bruker / Rigaku .raw and .xrdml.
    • Stacked or overlay plots with per-curve scale, offset, colour and labels; live normalisation to the visible 2θ range.
    • Processing — Savitzky–Golay, moving-average and Gaussian smoothing, arPLS background subtraction, with live preview.
    • Peak analysis — positions, d-spacings, FWHM and Scherrer crystallite-size estimates.
    • Export SVG / PNG / CSV or copy straight to the clipboard; save editable .pxrdproj sessions.
    • Undo up to 30 steps; processing never overwrites your source files.
